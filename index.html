Our story

One owner. One trowel. A whole lot of stone.

Y&C Landscaping & Masonry was founded — and is still owned — by Yovani. What started as a single retaining wall on a 90-degree Saturday has grown into a full crew that takes on property care, hardscape design, masonry, and fence work across the North Shore. The team has grown. The owner-on-site standards haven’t.

Today we’re a tight family-run crew based on Hamilton Street in Saugus, working across the North Shore. We swing the hammers ourselves. We believe good stonework is half engineering, half art — and 100% honest labor.

What we believe

Three rules we don’t break.

If it’s worth building, it’s worth over-building.

Deeper footings, stronger base, better drainage. You won’t see it — but you’ll feel it in 20 years.

Show up when we say we will.
